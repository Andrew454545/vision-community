VISION LOCAL SCENES

Process and search on your computer.

An invited preview for Windows PCs and Apple-silicon Macs. No account, subscription or Python installation is needed. These downloads are unsigned.

Start here

Download for Windows Download for Apple-silicon Mac
  1. Extract all the files from the ZIP.
  2. Open VISION Local Scenes.exe on Windows, or Start Local Scenes.command on Mac.
  3. Choose Check this computer, accept setup downloads, then Start / Resume.
  4. When finished, choose Save results and send the ZIP privately to the maintainer.
  5. After review, open your personal queue. A tiny live trial comes before a large queue.

Full simple instructions · Release notes and checksums

If computer security refuses the unsigned app, stop and ask for trusted help. Do not disable security.

Use it each day

Choose day and night speeds. Saved progress resumes after interruptions. Pause keeps work paused. Choose My library to search completed Scenes locally and export a map. Choose Choose storage folder before opening a new large queue.

Google must allow live imagery on your connection. Text searches use saved indexes without imagery requests. Your computer supplies electricity, internet and storage. Five million locations need about15.4 GB of vectors, plus metadata, setup and a searchable copy of the vectors.

Use your own MMA map

After the computer check, open My library → Add my own MMA map. Choose your exported JSON, then Prepare my private queue → Open my queue here. Set your speeds and choose Start. Use My saved queues to return to earlier personal maps. Opening stays paused until you choose Start. Panorama IDs are required. Your personal map results stay local. Community assignments still require maintainer review.

In My library, choose Use a photo from this computer to search with your own photo. Photo and text searches need no Google imagery requests.

Have the older hosted version?

Hosted processing and online search are paused. Keep your VISION folder, private account code and saved work. You do not need to reinstall the old version. Leave old automatic jobs paused.
