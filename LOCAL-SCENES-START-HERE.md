# VISION Local Scenes

This is an invited local processing preview. Get your personal Scene packet from
the maintainer before starting.

1. Extract the complete kit into a normal local folder. Keep all its files together.
2. On Windows, open **VISION Local Scenes.exe** and choose your Scene packet.
   On an Apple-silicon Mac, open **Start Local Scenes.command** and choose the packet.
3. Accept setup if you want to continue. The first setup downloads private Python
   and about 1 GB of Scene model files. It does not start indexing.
4. In the local browser window, choose **Start / Resume**.

Your own computer performs the work. No account, paid cloud processing or GitHub
Actions run is needed. Your electricity and internet data are still used.

**Pause** finishes the current group of up to 16 locations, then stops. **Exit**
does the same and closes the local pilot. Keep the computer plugged in.

Closing the browser tab leaves processing running. On Windows, choose **Reopen
pilot window** in the launcher to return to it. Close the launcher after using
Exit and waiting for the current group to finish.

The local window shows where your work is saved. Keep that whole folder and your
assigned packet. Reopen the same packet to resume. Do not share your packet with
another contributor; each person receives separate work.

After pausing or finishing, choose **Save results (.zip)** in the pilot window.
Your browser saves a portable copy of the finished groups. A partial backup is
clearly marked and excludes unfinished locations. Keep the original folder and
packet to resume; the backup does not replace them. Nothing is uploaded.

Your results stay on your computer. Uploads, shared search and credits come later.
Local results will need validation before entering the shared pool.

This preview is unsigned. If your computer refuses it, stop and ask the maintainer
for trusted installation help. Do not disable your computer's security settings.
If processing stops unexpectedly, keep the saved files and ask the maintainer
to review the local report.
