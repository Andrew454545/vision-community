# Seven-day accepted-work check

This is an operator release check. It tests the real contributor application,
service decisions and earned credit. Frozen-image replays and repeated unit
tests are useful preparation, but cannot start or complete this check.

## Before starting each operating system

Run Windows and Mac independently. A ready Windows run need not wait for the
Mac run, but both must pass before the corresponding release claim is made.

1. Pin the application, native runtime, models and approved parallel profiles.
   Each selected lane must have current device qualification under the exact
   policy used by its independent verifier. A diagnostic receipt is insufficient.
2. Confirm the required environment and database schema are ready. If the gate
   requires production, finish its full-data rehearsal and controlled migration
   first. A restricted genuine acceptance cohort need not open public admission;
   label its environment accurately and do not substitute a staging diagnostic
   for required production evidence. Confirm the service actually accepts fresh
   work in each lane and has eligible
   assignments. Objects need trusted current official Gen4 assignment evidence
   and real protected/quality authority. Never relabel old reference indexes or
   failed submissions as contributions.
3. Complete one fresh batch in each selected lane through the background worker.
   Independently confirm publication and the once-only credit transaction.
   Repeat its delivery to check that credit does not increase again.
4. Confirm online search works with genuinely earned credit and exact paid-result
   replay. Do not accumulate a large balance to avoid resolving early pricing.
5. Confirm saved account, checkpoint and delivery recovery, one native writer,
   automatic startup, the local day/night schedule and explicit CPU/RAM/storage
   budgets. Use medium by day and Maximum from 00:00 to 06:00 unless the tester
   saved another schedule. Maximum needs a verified parallel profile.
6. Fix a private evidence destination, retention allowance, end time and safe
   stop path. Keep raw logs, account codes and location data out of public reports.
   Run the contributor through the operating system's normal background startup;
   Codex and an open terminal must not be required.

Record the start only after these checks pass. Do not count time waiting for a
closed service, unavailable policy, qualification or the first accepted batch.
Never add synthetic credit to make the workload or its final search succeed.

## During the seven days

- Preserve durable batch/delivery receipts and independently reconcile accepted
  locations and credit with the service. Replays are recovery events, not new
  work. Record the application/policy/profile identities with the evidence.
- Record active time, scheduled rests, unavailable work and failure/retry time.
  Seven elapsed calendar days alone do not prove sustained accepted processing.
  Retain native CPU, memory and disk measurements with their measurement limits;
  sampled working sets do not establish exact shared physical memory or heat.
- Exercise real day/night boundaries and a settings change between batches.
  Exercise network loss, an interrupted native process, sleep/wake and an actual
  restart followed by sign-in. Verify recovery without overlapping writers,
  lost work, duplicate publication or another credit award.
- Do not extend a failed command's deadline or discard its report. A lasting
  trust/qualification failure stops that workload. Investigate it, retain the
  original evidence and start a separately identified run after fixing it.
- Sleep and shutdown pause computation. Record the pause and recovery; do not
  advertise continuous processing while the computer is asleep or powered off.

## Completion

After at least seven calendar days, reconcile both lanes, interruption evidence,
resource budgets, failure history and saved state on that operating system.
Confirm genuine accepted work throughout the planned active periods. A run with
no usable queue or only repeated diagnostic inputs remains incomplete.

Keep publication, credit and search checks separate: complete an online search
paid with genuinely earned banked credit and verify its exact retry does not
charge again. Do not count a disposable funded-search test as earned credit.

Publish aggregate results and retained-log checksums, including skips and gaps.
An operator review closes only the checks that the evidence establishes; a
successful soak does not replace signing, clean-device setup or full production
backup/recovery and capacity checks in [the launch plan](LAUNCH_PLAN.md).
