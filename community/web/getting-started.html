1. Open VISION

Download for Windows
  1. Right-click the downloaded ZIP. Choose Extract All, then Extract.
  2. Open the new folder, then VISION Community. Double-click Start VISION.cmd (or Start VISION).
  3. When asked, type Y and press Enter to allow downloads and imagery for your chosen work.

For Intel or AMD Windows PCs. No Python, Git or administrator password needed.

2. Follow the four buttons

  1. Set up this PC. Wait for the download (about 1 GB).
  2. Create account. Save your private code, then choose I have saved my code.
  3. Run the PC check. Wait for PC approved.
  4. When contributions open: choose Start helping. Keep VISION open, your PC awake and the internet connected.

This preview is closed. Stop after the PC check for now. You have done everything needed; the app will say when contributions open.

To stop, choose Pause after this batch. To return, open VISION and use your saved code.

More help

Automatic processing (when open)

When contributions are open, close VISION after setup and open Background VISION.cmd. Paste your saved account code once, choose the schedule, tick the permission box and choose Save and enable.

The default is Medium during the day and Maximum from midnight to 6 am. Close the controls when done; the worker continues. Reopen them to pause, resume or check status. Keep the extracted VISION folder.

Plug the PC into power. After a restart, sign into Windows to resume. Sleep or power-off stops processing until the PC is awake again. Network problems retry automatically; lasting failures or the storage limit pause work and keep its files. This preview still needs an extended endurance test.

Use your credits to search

Open the website → My account → enter your saved code. Describe a place, choose Run Search, then Download map.

Your account shows the current search price and your progress. Unused credits stay saved. Open your map in map-making.app.
