1

Set up this PC

Click the button, then wait. VISION downloads about 1 GB of files. A slow connection may take a while.

You do not need to install Python, type commands or use an administrator password.

2

Create an account

First time here? Choose Create account. No name or email needed.

I already have an account

Write down your private account code, or copy it somewhere safe. You need it to come back to your account. Never share it.

Keep your private code. You will also use it on the search website.

3

Check this PC

Click the button, then wait for “PC approved.” This short check makes sure your computer can do the work.

It tests 112 locations and may take a few minutes. Keep the internet connected.

4

Start helping

Click Start helping. Your computer finds locations and sends the results for a check. Accepted results earn credits.

Keep this page and the small starter window open. To stop, choose Pause after this batch and wait for “Processing paused.”
