# Automatic processing on Mac · preview

Use an Apple silicon Mac. Finish the guided setup and save your Community recovery code first.

1. Close VISION. Double-click **Background VISION.command**.
2. Paste your saved code once. The defaults are **Medium from 6 am** and **Maximum from midnight**.
3. Tick the contribution permission box and select **Save and enable**.

You can close the controls. Reopen them to **Pause after batch**, **Resume**, change your schedule or **Check status**.

Keep the Mac connected to power and internet. After a restart, sign in to resume. It cannot process while asleep, switched off or with the lid closed. A lasting failure pauses processing and keeps a private report. Resume does not clear an unresolved failure.

**Remove automatic startup** stops future automatic launches; it keeps your code and saved work. More free space or a larger allowance lets a full folder continue without deleting recovery files.

This preview supports the Scenes workflow. Mac production approval, Objects setup and long-duration device checks remain pending. Maximum uses the fastest approved profile; it does not enable an unapproved thread count.
